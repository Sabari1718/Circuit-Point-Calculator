Privacy Policy for Circuit Point

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

App Name: Circuit Point

Summary: Circuit Point is an offline-first electrical engineering utility and hardware specification catalog. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information or calculation data.

1. Information We Collect

Circuit Point does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). All calculation inputs, results, and favourite components are stored purely on your local device storage.

2. Local Device Storage

The application uses your device's local storage (such as SharedPreferences) solely to save:

  • Your saved calculation history (Power calculations and Resistor codes).
  • Your bookmarked favourite electronic components.
  • Your preferred theme mode (Light or Dark theme).

This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

3. Permissions

Circuit Point does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or file system storage.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or targeted advertising networks.

5. Children's Privacy

Circuit Point does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at: circuitpoint.app@gmail.com
